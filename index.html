Family life,

How have the timing and structure of Australian family life changed across generations? We begin with the changing age pattern of childbirth.

01 / Timing of parenthood

Childbirth has shifted into the thirties.

Since 2000, women aged 30–34 have had the highest birth rate. Between 1994 and 2024, rates fell in the early twenties while rising in the late thirties.

2000

The leading age group changes

Ages 30–34 overtook ages 25–29 and continued to lead in every year through 2024.

−56%

A lower rate at ages 20–24

From 69.9 to 30.6 births per 1,000 women between 1994 and 2024.

A higher rate at ages 35–39

From 41.2 to 66.6 births per 1,000 women over the same thirty years.

Rates cover all registered births, including subsequent births. They describe the population in each year, rather than tracking the same women or measuring age at first birth. Percentage changes compare 1994 with 2024 and are rounded to whole numbers.
